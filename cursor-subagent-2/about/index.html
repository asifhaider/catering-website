About Hearth & Plate

Hearth & Plate is a small homemade catering kitchen that cooks for gatherings, offices, and family tables. We prepare everything from scratch for pickup—so you get the warmth of home cooking without the scramble of hosting.

Our story

We started in a shared commercial kitchen with one roasting pan and a short list of weekend orders. Neighbors asked for Sunday trays, then friends asked for office lunches, and the weekly menus grew from there.

Today we still cook in small batches. Each weekday has its own menu—protein dishes, vegetarian mains, and sides—so you can build a tray that fits your table without settling for a one-size-fits-all buffet.

How pickup works

  1. Browse the menu for a pickup date at least 2 days and at most 2 weeks ahead.
  2. Choose dishes and set portions for 6 to 30 people per item.
  3. Check out with your contact details and preferred payment method.
  4. Pick up your order at the scheduled time—ready to warm and serve.

What we cook

  • Roasted meats and fish with bright, seasonal sauces
  • Vegetarian mains that stand on their own, not as an afterthought
  • Simple sides meant to travel well and reheat cleanly

What we value

  • Scratch cooking with familiar ingredients you can pronounce
  • Clear menus by day so planning is predictable
  • Pickup-first service that keeps catering approachable
